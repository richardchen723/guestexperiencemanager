How your numbers work

Revenue: USD accommodation charges after included standard room discounts, before management fees and operating expenses. Cleaning, taxes, deposits and other fees are excluded. Charges are spread evenly across stay nights, excluding checkout. Separate refunds and resolutions cannot reliably be assigned to room charges; affected stays are flagged and these adjustments belong in finalized accounting. Cancelled stays are excluded. A “Partial” value is the verified subtotal, with missing records disclosed—not a complete total.

Occupancy: Guest nights divided by sellable nights, excluding known blocks and owner stays. Historical calendars are backfilled from Hostaway. Where availability history is missing, the explicitly labeled calendar-night percentage uses all calendar nights, without assuming unknown nights were available. Overlapping reservations make occupancy unavailable.

Estimates: Existing bookings plus potential new bookings at current calendar rates. We use seasonal booking pickup at matched lead times from the same month and its adjacent months in up to three previous years. When that sample is small, recent open-to-booked conversion provides a fallback. Each needs at least 30 eligible historical nights. Short gaps below minimum stay are excluded. Where at least 30 historical reservations support it, the range also allows for cancellations among bookings held at similar lead times. Otherwise existing bookings are assumed to remain, and that limitation is shown. Ranges describe planning scenarios, not guaranteed income, calibrated confidence or owner payouts.

Comparisons: The same properties and month last year. Percentage changes are withheld when the full comparison is incomplete. Future estimates compare with prior completed accommodation charges, not historical booking pace. Data older than 48 hours is flagged and forecasts withheld.

Property care: Cleaning and maintenance status records, with private ticket text removed. Completed counts require a recorded completion transition in the selected month; open counts show the recorded status at month-end or today. Requests are explicitly published by staff; responding records a decision and does not make a payment.

Statements: Published finalized bookkeeping summaries for exactly your properties. Their accounting basis can differ from accommodation charges. Versions stay fixed after publication; payment is unconfirmed.
