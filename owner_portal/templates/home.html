Definitions & forecast details

Revenue: USD accommodation charges, before management fees and operating expenses. Cleaning, taxes, deposits and other fees are excluded. Confirmed stays are allocated by night to their stay month, excluding checkout. Cancellations are excluded; unresolved discounts or incomplete source amounts are withheld.

Occupancy: Guest nights divided by sellable physical-unit nights. Blocked nights, owner stays and imported calendar blocks are excluded and disclosed separately. Unclassified or conflicting nights make occupancy unavailable.

Estimates: Confirmed revenue plus potential new bookings at current calendar rates. The planning range uses observed open-to-booked conversion at similar lead times over the last 90 completed nights, with at least 30 observations per lead time. Short gaps below the current minimum stay are excluded. This initial model is not seasonally adjusted or backtested; it assumes existing bookings remain. Prices, cancellations, discounts and stay restrictions can change the outcome. It is not a payout estimate or a guarantee.

Comparisons: The same completed month last year, only within your reporting access. Future estimates are compared with prior actuals, not historical booking pace. Missing history is never shown as zero. Source data more than 48 hours old is flagged and forecasts are withheld.

Statements: Explicitly published, finalized bookkeeping summaries. Their accounting basis can differ from accommodation revenue. Versions stay fixed after publication; payment is unconfirmed.
